    How to search

    Search types

    Any word
    Pages that contain at least one of your words.
    All words
    Pages that contain every word, anywhere on the page.
    Exact phrase
    The words side by side, in order. Line breaks don’t matter.
    Near
    All words within a set number of words of each other.
    Boolean
    AND OR NOT and ( ), e.g. (stall OR spin) AND recovery NOT training
    Regex
    A JavaScript regular expression, e.g. \b\d{3}-\d{4}\b

    Works in every type

    "two words"
    Quotes keep words together as one term.
    -word
    Leave out pages containing it.
    hydraul*
    A star matches any letters.
    Aa ab
    Match case · whole words only.
